# Valsoft Dataset Observations

This document summarizes the forensic findings and transaction observations generated by running the upgraded multi-signal fraud intelligence engine against the unlabelled alternative dataset transactions.csv.

## 1. Dataset Overview

The alternative dataset consists of unlabelled transactions with a custom schema tracking transaction metadata, device IDs, and IP addresses. 

* **Total Records**: 1,000 transactions
* **Active Cardholders**: 50 unique cards (card_000 to card_049)
* **Average Volume per Cardholder**: 20 transactions
* **Observation Period**: April 25, 2026, to May 11, 2026
* **Total Anomaly Flags Raised**: 24 flags

### Schema Mapping
To execute the audit, the raw columns were dynamically mapped to the intelligence engine's unified data model:
* **card_id** mapped to **employee_id** and **employee_name**
* **timestamp** parsed to **transaction_date**
* **amount** mapped to **amount_usd** and **amount_cad** (1:1 ratio)
* **merchant_category** translated to representative industry MCC codes (gas to 5541, restaurant to 5812, utilities to 4814, subscription to 5734, online_retail to 5943, grocery to 5411, atm to 6011, entertainment to 7832)

## 2. Anomaly Breakdown by Pattern Type

The analysis isolated 24 separate statistical, peer, and merchant concentration flags.

### Duplicate Expenses (2 Detections)
Identified exact double-swipe charges submitted within a narrow chronological window. Both detections occurred under cardholder card_007 at the RBC ATM vendor:
* **Duplicate Swipe 1**: Submitted two identical charges of $40.00 USD at RBC ATM 1 day apart.
* **Duplicate Swipe 2**: Submitted two identical charges of $40.00 USD at RBC ATM 2 day(s) apart.
This pattern indicates possible card-testing or repeated automated cash withdrawals.

### Merchant Spend Concentration (3 Detections)
Measured via the Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI) on spend shares. The engine isolated three cardholders funneling a disproportionate share of their total budget to single merchants:
* **card_009**: HHI = 0.47 (Directs 67% of total spend to QuickPay Online)
* **card_018**: HHI = 0.44 (Directs 62% of total spend to Gift Card Mall)
* **card_020**: HHI = 0.42 (Directs 61% of total spend to Apple Store)
Spend concentration at online payment gateways and retail gift cards is a common vector for cash-out fraud.

### Peer Group Benchmarking (5 Detections)
Benchmarked total cardholder spend against the peer average ($1,364.51 CAD) after filtering out capital outlier transactions above $10,000:
* **card_016**: Spent $5,444.49 CAD (3.8 standard deviations above peer average)
* **card_018**: Spent $4,700.20 CAD (3.1 standard deviations above peer average)
* **card_020**: Spent $4,308.76 CAD (2.8 standard deviations above peer average)
* **card_021**: Spent $2,379.22 CAD (1.0 standard deviations above peer average)
* **card_037**: Spent $2,332.58 CAD (0.9 standard deviations above peer average)

### Spending Velocity Spikes (6 Detections)
Analyzed personal baseline deviation in rolling 7-day spending windows. Six cardholders triggered flags for dramatic velocity bursts:
* **card_049**: Spiked 21.5 standard deviations above personal baseline.
* **card_029**: Spiked 12.4 standard deviations above personal baseline.
* **card_040**: Spiked 7.3 standard deviations above personal baseline.
* **card_048**: Spiked 5.3 standard deviations above personal baseline.

### MCC Amount Outliers (8 Detections)
Isolated transactions whose individual amount was an extreme statistical outlier compared to all charges in that category (z-score > 5.0). These represent single massive purchases that distort normal budget limits, primarily under cardholders card_007, card_048, card_019, card_032, card_041, and card_046.

## 4. Key Takeaways and Recommendations

1. **Card-Testing and Automation Risk**: The duplication of ATM cash-out charges on card_007 indicates possible automated scripts or physical card cloning. Immediate review of ATM transaction policies is recommended.
2. **Channel and Gateway Controls**: Multiple cardholders are funneling budget to online merchant processors (QuickPay Online) and retail cards (Gift Card Mall). Corporate policies should restrict high HHI concentration spend on non-procurement vendors.
3. **Budget Leakage**: Cardholders card_016 and card_018 represent massive operational spend deviations ($3.8\sigma$ and $3.1\sigma$) relative to the corporate baseline. Benchmarking spending against a peer group mean proved effective in isolating these silent leaks.
